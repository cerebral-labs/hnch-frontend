What you'll do

  • Complete hunches in Ping — up to 5 count each day toward your score.
  • Invite friends — get friends to join Ping, and earn points when they activate.
  • Stay on the list — each week, complete at least 3 hunches and have at least 2 friends onboard.

Submit weekly feedback to qualify that week and unlock invites. Target: 50+ new admitted members each week, building a 100+ founding community.

Daily routine

Keep it simple: complete hunches and bring friends in.

  • Complete hunches — 1 point each; up to 5 count per day (max 35 hunch points per week).
  • Invite friends — each friend who completes a hunch on at least 3 separate days counts once (+10), in the week they hit that third day.
  • Weekly feedback — required to qualify for scoring that week and to unlock invites. Feedback itself does not add points.
  • Stay on the list — each week, complete at least 3 hunches and have at least 2 friends onboard. An invite counts when the friend joins Ping, not when you send it. Miss either minimum and you do not remain on the list that week (your seat may return to the invite pool).

How scoring works

Complete hunches and invite friends. Your total is what counts — still two boards: Personal and Crew.

Personal

Your score = your hunch points + your activated invite points.

  • 1 point per completed hunch. Up to 5 count each day · max 35 hunch points per week.
  • +10 per activated invite: a friend who completes a hunch on at least 3 separate days. Each friend counts once, in the week they complete their third qualifying day.
  • Highest total wins. Ties go to whoever completed a hunch on more days that week.

Crew

Your group’s score = the group’s combined hunch points + the group’s combined invite points. Groups compete; the in-house leader of a winning group receives the crew prize for recruiting, following up, and supporting members — not every member, and not the leader’s solo usage alone.

  • Same point rules: 1 per hunch (5/day, 35/week per person) and +10 per activated invite.
  • Ties go to the group with more member-days where someone completed a hunch.

Submit your weekly feedback to qualify for both boards that week and unlock invites. Feedback does not add points.

Stay on the list: each week, complete at least 3 hunches and have at least 2 friends onboard. An invite counts when the friend joins Ping, not when you send it. This weekly minimum is separate from scoring points. Miss either and you do not remain on the list that week.

Prizes

Crew: groups compete; the in-house leader of each winning group earns the crew prize for recruitment, follow-up and member support. Leaders invite friends, check their activity and status, and help them participate and share feedback.

How you earn

One total score: hunch points + activated invite points. Still two boards — Personal and Crew. Weekly feedback qualifies you and unlocks invites; it does not add points.
Personal board Your hunches + your activated invites. Weekly: 1st $50, 2nd $25, 3rd $15, and 4th–6th $10 each in Amazon / Visa gift cards, starting Week 2.
Crew board Group’s combined hunches + group’s combined activated invites. The winning group’s in-house leader receives the crew prize, not each member. Weekly top 3 groups’ leaders receive $50 / $25 / $15 Amazon / Visa gift cards, starting Week 2.
Point rules 1 point per completed hunch · up to 5/day · max 35 hunch points/week. +10 per friend who completes a hunch on at least 3 separate days (counts once, in that week). Ties: more hunch-days (personal) or more member-days with a completed hunch (crew).

Ground rules

  • Complete hunches in Ping and invite friends who qualify.
